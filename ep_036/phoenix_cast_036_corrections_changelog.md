## Episode summary
"Potent Potables" — an unscripted, hosts-only episode that runs through three loosely connected topics:
1. **Why you should come work in cyber** — a recruitment pitch for both military and civilian sides of the cybersecurity workforce, plus objection handling (age, technology, management vs. IC).
2. **PME (Professional Military Education)** — a walkthrough of USMC officer PME (EWS, Command & Staff, JPME II, Top Level School / Marine Corps War College) and a comparison to the lack of equivalent training pipelines in the civilian world.
3. **Conclusions you should (and shouldn't) draw from this podcast** — push back on simplistic takeaways like "rewrite all software," "all cloud all the time," and "go off-grid"; ends on "don't reuse bad passwords, don't click hyperlinks" and "man, it's complicated."
